A restaurant inside a working hotel

Our kitchen sits within the Sanskar Grand hotel, so the same cooks feed city diners, families and overnight guests.

Breakfast is parathas, poha and chole bhature. Dinner runs from dal makhani and paneer tikka to Indo-Chinese. Every dish is vegetarian, generously portioned and priced inclusive of GST.

  • 100% vegetarian kitchen
  • In-room dining
  • GST-inclusive billing
  • Open 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM
  • Banquet and party hall
  • Family and group friendly
