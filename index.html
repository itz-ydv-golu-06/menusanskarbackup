Our Story

A grand Room inside a premium stay

Set within the Sanskar Grand hotel on Jaipur's 200 Feet Bypass Road, our kitchen serves guests and city diners alike — from hearty Indian breakfasts to slow-cooked dal makhani and tandoor-fresh breads, right through to late-night comfort food.

Every dish on our menu is 100% vegetarian, prepared with the same care whether it reaches a hotel room or a table in our dining room.
