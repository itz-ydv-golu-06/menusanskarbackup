Since Day One · A Legacy of Hospitality

A grand table inside a premium stay

Set within the Sanskar Grand hotel on Jaipur's 200 Feet Bypass Road, our kitchen serves guests and city diners alike.

From hearty Indian breakfasts at sunrise to slow-simmered dal makhani and tandoor-fresh breads at midnight, every plate leaves the pass with the same promise — pure vegetarian, generously portioned, and made with intention.

Whether it reaches a hotel room on a silver tray or a candle-lit table in our dining room, the care never changes. That is the Sanskar Grand way.
